First release.

- Honk at a garage door to store the car; type its plate on the spot, or leave it blank for a random one picked by your LibertyCityPlates rules
- F6 on foot at the door: pick a car and it comes out repaired and clean, with its plate
- 20 spaces (configurable), wrecked or lost cars come back, garage cars returning with a saved game get their plate back
- Set up for the Lost clubhouse in Acter; up to 8 doors

Install: put `LCGarage.asi` and `LCGarage.ini` in the game folder. Needs an ASI loader (FusionFix has one) and LibertyCityPlates for plates.
